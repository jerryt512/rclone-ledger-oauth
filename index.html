rclone-ledger

個人帳本的唯讀備份用戶端 · A read-only backup client for a personal ledger

這是什麼 · What it is

rclone-ledger 是只供擁有者本人使用的 Google OAuth 用戶端。它讓擁有者電腦上的 rclone，以唯讀權限把擁有者自己 Google 雲端硬碟中的一份試算表匯出成 XLSX，存到擁有者自己的電腦做為備份。

rclone-ledger is a Google OAuth client used only by its owner. It lets rclone on the owner's computer export one spreadsheet from the owner's own Google Drive to XLSX, read-only, and keep it on that computer as a backup.

它不做什麼 · What it does not do

不對外提供服務，沒有其他使用者；不寫入、修改或刪除任何雲端檔案；不把資料傳給任何第三方。

It offers no service to anyone else and has no other users. It never writes, changes or deletes cloud files, and sends no data to any third party.
